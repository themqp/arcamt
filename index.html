Designing spaces

Muhammad Tijani Abubakar is a results-driven Architect and Construction Manager with over 6 years of hands-on experience spanning affordable housing, residential developments, and large-scale construction projects across Ghana and the United States.

Holding both a Master of Science in Civil Engineering and a Bachelor of Science in Architecture, Muhammad brings a rare combination of structural rigor and design sensibility to every project — translating client visions into functional, eco-conscious, and aesthetically compelling built environments.

Currently pursuing a Master of Construction Science at Texas A&M University and active with AIA Central Oklahoma, he continues to champion sustainable architecture and community-focused design.
